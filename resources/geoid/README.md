# EGM96 geoid undulation grid

`egm96_1deg.bin` holds the EGM96 geoid height above the WGS84 ellipsoid on a 1 degree grid, used to compare
heights above mean sea level with heights above the ellipsoid.

- 181 rows from 90N to 90S, 360 columns from 0E eastwards
- Little endian signed 16 bit integers in decimeters

Generated by sampling the 15 minute grid `egm96-15.pgm` from the GeographicLib geoid distribution
(<https://geographiclib.sourceforge.io/C++/doc/geoid.html>), which is derived from the EGM96 model published by
the US National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency. EGM96 is public domain. Bilinear interpolation of this grid is
within 0.1 m of the 15 minute grid for half of all locations, 1.7 m for 99 % and 12.6 m at worst.
